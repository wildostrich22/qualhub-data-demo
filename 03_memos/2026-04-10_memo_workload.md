# Memo: The hidden cost

**Date:** 10 April 2026
**Interviews coded so far:** 12 of 12

Almost every farmer who reported steadier income also described longer hours.

This suggests a trade-off. Diversification brings stability, but the cost is paid in time and stress.

**Decision:** keep "Income stability" and "Workload strain" as separate codes, but report them together in the findings as one theme.
