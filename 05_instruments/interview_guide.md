# Interview Guide (demo)

Interviews lasted around 45 minutes.

1. Can you tell me about your farm and how long you have run it?
2. What new business activity have you started?
3. What made you decide to start it?
4. What helped you get it started?
5. What made it difficult?
6. How has it changed the farm business?
7. How has it changed your day-to-day life?
8. Is there anything else you would like to add?
